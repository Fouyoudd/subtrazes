Privacy Policy

Your data, explained clearly.

This page explains what Subtraz accesses, what it stores, and how Gmail Receipt Auto-Fill works.

Last updated: 27 May 2026
Gmail is optional. You can manually add subscriptions and use Subtraz without connecting Gmail. Gmail access is only used when you choose Receipt Auto-Fill.

1. What Subtraz does

Subtraz helps users track subscriptions, trials, billing dates, and renewal reminders. Its optional Receipt Auto-Fill feature checks for supported subscription receipts in a connected Gmail account and prepares subscription details for the user to review and save.

2. Google account and Gmail access

When you connect Gmail for Receipt Auto-Fill, Subtraz requests basic Google account information and read-only Gmail access.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

Read-only Gmail access allows Subtraz to view Gmail messages for the purpose described on this page. Subtraz does not request permission to send, edit, or delete your Gmail messages.

3. What Gmail messages are checked

Subtraz uses Gmail access to search recent messages that match supported subscription services and receipt or billing terms. This may include terms such as receipt, payment, subscription, renewal, invoice, charged, premium, or pro.

For matching messages, Subtraz may process the sender, subject, and email content to identify subscription details.

4. What data is extracted

From a supported subscription receipt or payment email, Subtraz may extract:

  • Subscription service name
  • Category
  • Amount shown in the receipt
  • Billing cycle, such as monthly or annual
  • Billing date or estimated next billing date
  • Receipt message identifier used to prevent duplicate alerts

5. What Subtraz stores

To provide Receipt Auto-Fill and renewal alerts, Subtraz currently stores some or all of the following:

  • Your Subtraz account identifier
  • Your connected Google account identifier and Gmail address
  • Google OAuth tokens needed to keep Gmail connected
  • Extracted subscription details used for auto-fill and reminders
  • Receipt message identifiers used to avoid sending duplicate alerts
  • Private auto-fill link records and whether a link has been opened
  • Browser push notification subscription data, when you enable push alerts

6. Where processing happens

Gmail receipt checking is processed on the Subtraz server through the Gmail API. It is not processed only inside your browser.

Subtraz uses service providers needed to run these features:

Google Gmail API Connects your Gmail account with read-only access.
Supabase Stores app records, connected account data, and feature data.
Resend Sends Subtraz email alerts containing auto-fill links.
Browser push services Sends push alerts only when you enable them.

7. Private auto-fill links

When Subtraz finds a supported receipt, it may send you a private Add to Subtraz link. The link is designed to open the add form with detected subscription details already filled in.

Private auto-fill links expire after 24 hours. During the beta period, related records may remain stored unless you ask for their deletion.

8. Disconnecting Gmail and removing access

The Disconnect Gmail button inside Subtraz removes the saved Gmail connection record associated with your signed-in Subtraz account.

At this stage of the beta, disconnecting inside Subtraz does not automatically remove Google permission from your Google Account. To fully stop Google account access, you should also remove Subtraz from your Google Account connected apps settings.

Imported subscription records and previous alert records are not automatically deleted when Gmail is disconnected.

9. Data deletion requests

During beta testing, you may request deletion of your connected Gmail records, imported subscription records, and related Subtraz account data by contacting:

support@subtraz.top

10. Changes to this policy

If Subtraz changes how Google user data is accessed, used, or stored, this page will be updated before the new use is made available to users.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to:

support@subtraz.top

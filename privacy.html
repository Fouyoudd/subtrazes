Privacy Policy

Subtraz is a subscription tracking tool. This policy explains what information is used when you create an account, track subscriptions, or connect Gmail for Receipt Auto-Fill.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is only for account login. It creates or opens your Subtraz account.

Gmail access

Gmail access is optional and separate. Subtraz uses read-only Gmail access only to look for subscription-related receipts and help fill in subscription details. You can track subscriptions manually without Gmail.

Saved subscription data

Saved subscription data may include service name, amount, billing date, cycle, category, and project.

What Subtraz does not save

Subtraz does not save Gmail passwords, bank details, or card details.

User control

You can request deletion or disconnect Gmail. For help, email support@subtraz.top.
